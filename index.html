A FEW HELPFUL DETAILS

Good to know.

Quick answers before you visit or place a demo order.

Can I change my order after adding items?＋

Yes. Return to the menu and add more items. On the bill page, you can also change quantities or remove items before printing your sample bill.

Can I request vegetarian options?＋

Use the menu categories and dish descriptions to explore options. This sample menu is for demonstration; please confirm ingredients and dietary needs with a real restaurant.

Does this website accept real payments?＋

No. This is a front-end student project. The order summary and bill are generated in your browser; no payment is collected and no order is sent to a restaurant.

Can I print my order bill?＋

Yes. Choose “Print / Save as PDF” on the bill page, then select your printer or “Save as PDF” in the browser print dialog.

How do I make a table reservation?＋

Fill in the reservation form on this page. It will show a local confirmation for demonstration, but it does not reserve a real table.
